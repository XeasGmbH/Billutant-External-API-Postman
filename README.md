The project Billutant-External-API-Postman is for an understandable explanation for the api of the Billutant software.
To use it you will need:
 . a Billutant account: billutant.com
 . Postman: https://www.postman.com/downloads/
 . this git project: https://github.com/XeasGmbH/Billutant-External-API-Postman

manual:
1. download all you need.
2. open Postman.
3. drag and drop the two files: allWorks.postam_environment.json and Billutant External API.postman_collection.json to over postman.
4. click on Import.
5. log in into your Billutant account.
6. open the left side bar.
7. click on Settings.
8. click on Features.
9. activate External invoice API.
10. click on API.
11. click on Create token.
12. make up any name.
13. click on Create token.
14. click on Copy.
15. switch to Postman
16. open the left side bar.
17. open ENVIRONMENTS.
19. click on the tick beside allWorks to activate it.
18. click on allWorks.
19. click in the empty Value field fo the authToken.
20. press ctrl+v.
21. open COLLECTIONS.
22. click on Billutant External API.
23. click on Run.
24. click on Start run.
Now you have tested your API and see what works and what doesn't.
